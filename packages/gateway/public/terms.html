2. Local-First Operation & Data Privacy

NOVA.ai is designed as a State-Isolated Service-Oriented Architecture (SISOA) running locally on your computer. We value your privacy and security:

  • Zero Cloud Telemetry: Your prompts, logs, files, and chatbot conversation histories remain strictly on your local device database.

  • Credentials Encryption: System environment configurations and API integration keys (e.g. HubSpot tokens, Telegram keys) are saved on your local storage and encrypted using AES-256-GCM.
